Dataset Selection & Preparation

To train our models, we utilized the SARC (Sarcasm on Reddit) dataset. This dataset is highly reliable as it relies on self-reported sarcasm (users explicitly appending the /s tag to their posts).

  • Class Balancing: The original dataset contained over a million records. To prevent severe hardware bottlenecks and model bias, we extracted a perfectly balanced sample of 100,000 records (50,000 sarcastic, 50,000 normal).
  • Preprocessing Pipeline: Real-world internet data is noisy. We built a Python pipeline to strip URLs, remove subreddit references, eliminate special characters, and convert all text to lowercase before vectorization.

Model Architecture: The Sarcasm Challenge

Sarcasm is fundamentally difficult for natural language processing because it is not about the words themselves, but the order and context of those words. This required testing two distinct mathematical approaches:

Model Strategy Text Processing Outcome & Insight
Ensemble (Random Forest) TF-IDF Vectorization Achieved 65.18% accuracy. Struggled with a high False Negative rate (missing nearly 5,000 sarcastic comments). Because TF-IDF ignores word order, the model had to base decisions purely on sarcastic trigger words.
Neural Network (LSTM) Word Embeddings Highly Successful (69.74%). LSTMs read sequences and remember past context. This allowed the model to understand structural sarcasm (e.g., positive premise, negative punchline).

Optimization & Model Selection

Before finalizing our architecture, we attempted several advanced ensemble techniques to push past the 65% baseline. Documenting these failures provided critical insights into the nature of sarcasm detection:

  • Voting Classifier Attempt: We attempted to combine Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, and Random Forest. However, holding three distinct algorithms and a massive TF-IDF matrix simultaneously exceeded cloud hardware limits, resulting in a system crash.
  • AdaBoost Attempt: To fix the hardware crash, we utilized AdaBoost, which is RAM-friendly. It performed poorly (56.83% accuracy). AdaBoost relies on single-feature splits ("stumps"). This proved that sarcasm cannot be detected by looking at single words in isolation; it requires multi-word context.
  • Final Selection: Due to the contextual limits of AdaBoost and the hardware constraints of the Voting Classifier, we locked in the original Random Forest as our official Ensemble model, as it provided the best balance of multi-feature analysis and stability.

2. Overfitting Prevention (Early Stopping)

When training the LSTM, the model began memorizing the training data around Epoch 4. We implemented an EarlyStopping callback to halt training the moment validation accuracy plateaued, ensuring the model generalizes well to new text.
